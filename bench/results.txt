Notes per tier:

baseline:  -O3 -Wall -fopenmp -ffast-math, Threads-only link
tier1:     +-march=native -funroll-loops -DNDEBUG -flto -fopenmp linked
           Marginal speedup. Most hot code lives in templated headers, so
           cross-TU LTO has little to do; -march=native cannot really
           vectorise because of the nested-vector data layout (deferred to
           Tier 5). Diff vs baseline: BSE outputs (fg, b) match within
           rtol=1e-6 atol=1e-7. WTI Delta_A files (w_file_idx_1,
           w_z0_file_idx_1) excluded — catastrophic cancellation.

tier4:     Hoist the two lInterpolator2d objects out of the qint2d
           integrand lambda in a_iteration_step (was reconstructed 1024×
           per (i,k,z,j) tuple), and add lInterpolator2d::unchecked(y,z)
           that skips the bounds tolerance / clamp / throw block. The
           y/z arguments come from Gauss-Legendre / Chebyshev zeros that
           are strictly interior to the grid by construction, so the
           safety net was paranoia.
           Massive jump — total runtime drops 61% versus the baseline.
           The slow combos (nodse_pv, dse_*) collapse to roughly the
           fast combo's wall time, suggesting that the 1024-rebinds-per-
           integrand penalty was scaling with BSE iteration count and
           was responsible for most of the variation between flag
           combos. precalculate_K_kernel is now the dominant phase,
           which is exactly Tier 5's target (flatten the 6-deep
           ijtens2_double storage).

tier3:     Refactor G constructor: compute kp_sq, km_sq, sigma_v, m_kp,
           m_km, sigma_m, delta_m, the ten g_xx tensor components once
           per (k², z, q²) point; the (i,j) inner loop now does only the
           switch dispatch via a static select_g_snake helper. Replace
           4×4 std::vector<std::vector<complex>> with std::array — drops
           3 heap allocs per G construction. Old kernel_G/kernel_G_snake
           and the sigma_v/quark members are gone.
           ~1.5% on top of Tier 2 (G is only used in b_iteration_step,
           which is small relative to precalculate_K_kernel).

tier2:     Hoist k², k, sqrt(1-z²) tables out of precalculate_K_kernel;
           rewrite K constructor to take precomputed sqrt(k²·k'²), s_z,
           s_z', k, k'; replace K::isZeroIndex if-chain with constexpr
           144-bool table; collapse(3) → collapse(5) on the K-kernel
           parallel for. nodse combos pick up another ~2% on top of
           tier1; dse combos drift up by 1-3% (within timing noise — DSE
           code is unchanged).
           Honest read: -O3 -ffast-math + -march=native already CSE'd
           most of these per-y invariants, so explicit hoisting mostly
           clarifies code rather than gains FLOPs. Real wins are queued
           up for Tier 4 (hoist lInterpolator2d construction, skip
           bounds checks in known-safe inner loops) and Tier 5 (flatten
           the nested-vector storage so SIMD can finally engage).
